## 🎯 What I Do

I build AI systems that understand documents, images, and human behavior—with a focus on **fairness, accessibility, and cultural preservation**.

My work bridges **computer vision**, **cognitive science**, and **responsible AI** to ensure technology serves everyone, especially marginalized communities whose histories risk being erased or misrepresented by biased systems.

Currently researching: **How do we make AI "read" historical documents without perpetuating the biases baked into existing systems?**

---

## 🔬 Research Highlights

### Layout-Aware OCR for Black Digital Archives
*Accepted at IEEE ISTAS 2025*

Developed unsupervised evaluation methods to assess OCR systems on historically significant Black archives—addressing a critical gap where existing benchmarks fail to capture the unique challenges of cultural heritage materials.

**The problem:** Standard OCR systems are trained on modern, standardized documents. Historical Black archives? Not so much.

### Cognitive Framework for AI Bias Detection
*Under Review*

Designed a novel study framework combining cognitive science and sociocultural theory to detect and analyze bias in AI systems. Synthesized 40+ sources and trained research assistants to implement the protocol.
